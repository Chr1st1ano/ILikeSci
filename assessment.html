Science Laboratory & Safety Practice Checklist

Put a check mark (✓) for safe/good practices and cross mark (✗) for unsafe practices

DepEd Science Rubric
1. Wearing safety goggles and gloves when handling science chemicals or soil samples.
2. Looking directly at the bright midday sun without protective eclipse glasses.
3. Washing hands with soap thoroughly after touching plant leaves, soils, or rocks.
4. Tasting or sniffing unknown substances in the laboratory without teacher permission.
5. Using an umbrella and wearing wide-brimmed hats during hot outdoor field studies.
6. Immediately notifying the teacher when glass or equipment is cracked or broken.
