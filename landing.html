Digital Flash Quizzes

Quick recitation assessments with automatic scoring. Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels with fair student suggestion system.

Interactive Games

Dance, sing, and group activities with timer and point tracking. Gamified learning that keeps students engaged.

TV Presenter Mode

Dual-screen display like Canva. Control from your laptop while showing content on external monitor or TV for the class.

Real-time Scoreboard

Live ranking system with fairness alerts. Track student progress and encourage healthy competition.

Curriculum Lessons

Import and organize lesson plans with slide-by-slide delivery. Support for MATATAG K to 10 Curriculum.

Cloud Sync

Backup and restore data to MySQL database. Offline support with automatic sync when online.
